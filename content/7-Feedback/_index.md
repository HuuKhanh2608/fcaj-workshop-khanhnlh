> This section shares my personal feedback on my experience in the First Cloud AI Journey program, to help the FCAJ team improve, based on the following areas:

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is friendly and open, and FCAJ members are willing to help when I run into difficulties. Working and attending events at the AWS office in Bitexco Financial Tower felt professional, and the learning community is lively, with many interns studying and exchanging ideas through AWS Study Group and community sessions. I think more social or team-bonding activities would help interns get to know each other better.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The program is mostly self-directed, and there is no fixed step-by-step roadmap, so I gradually learned to plan my own path through the labs. Mentors let me try and work through problems myself before giving an answer, which made me more independent and better at finding information. A clearer roadmap at the start would help newcomers get on track faster.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
Cloud computing is fairly new compared with what I study in Software Engineering, but it is very useful for my career direction. Learning how applications run on AWS infrastructure helps me see the whole picture, from writing code to deploying and operating it.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The structured hands-on labs from Module 01 to Module 07 let me practice on a real AWS account. The events gave me the chance to hear from industry practitioners, writing technical blogs trained me to read AWS documentation and explain it to others, and preparing my own workshop pushed me to design, deploy, and estimate costs for a complete solution.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
People openly share knowledge, materials, and lab experience with each other. I attended events and the watch party together with my FCAJ teammates, and interns are respected and listened to as part of the community.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The schedule is flexible: I book office visits and arrange my own study time. Interns can attend AWS events and use the lab system and videos for free, and the completion requirements (workshop, blogs, events, and office visits) are published clearly from the start.

---

### Additional Questions

- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?  
  Doing hands-on labs on a real AWS account: building the resources myself, checking the results, and cleaning everything up afterward.
- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?  
  A clearer learning roadmap, regular review sessions with mentors, more team-bonding activities, and earlier guidance on the workshop requirements and how to choose a topic.
- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?  
  Yes, for friends who want to pursue cloud and can study on their own, because the program requires a lot of self-discipline.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?  
  A weekly checklist of knowledge and labs to complete, small study groups each followed by a mentor, a complete sample workshop with a step-by-step walkthrough, and a mid-term demo day to get feedback early.
- Would you like to continue this program in the future?  
  Yes. I want to keep following the learning path and work toward an AWS certification.
- Any other comments (free sharing):  
  Thank you to the FCAJ program, the mentors, and my fellow interns for accompanying me throughout this internship.
